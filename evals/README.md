# Evaluation contributions

[Chinese](README.zh-CN.md) · [Verification](../docs/VALIDATION.md)

Help test what a person can actually do with OpenXSys. Share the prompt, AI tool, model, version and outcome, with the files needed to understand the result.

Useful cases include a first installation, one question about a company or industry, a method change, a finished deliverable and continuation in a new session. For document loading, include the raw file-read calls and how the documents influenced the work.

Share a successful deliverable through the [gallery](../gallery/README.md). [Report a miss](misses/README.md) with what you asked, what happened and what you expected. Provider review contributions follow the [review guide](reviews/README.md).
