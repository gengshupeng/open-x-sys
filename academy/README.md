# Academy

[Chinese](README.zh-CN.md)

Where people learn systems thinking by using it. The Academy follows the journey: See, Explain, Anticipate, Focus, Decide, Create, Improve.

**Status.** This page describes the Academy. Lessons have not been written yet. The method pages in `skills/open-x-sys/methods/` are the current source for each method, and learn mode and the Playground tour are the current ways to learn.

## A lesson

One lesson per method. Each has:

1. **The idea in plain words.**
2. **A famous real case**, with its source and date.
3. **The person behind it**, and the primary source to read.
4. **An exercise**, which is a prompt used on something in the learner's own life.

No invented quotations. Every case is checked against its source before a lesson is published.

## A graded reading list

Start here: Donella Meadows, *Thinking in Systems* (2008); Eliyahu Goldratt, *The Goal* (1984); Peter Senge, *The Fifth Discipline* (1990).

Then: Taiichi Ohno, *Toyota Production System* (1978); W. Edwards Deming, *Out of the Crisis* (1986); Howard Marks, *The Most Important Thing* (2011); Jane Jacobs, *The Death and Life of Great American Cities* (1961); Herbert Simon, *The Sciences of the Artificial* (1969).

Deeper: John Sterman, *Business Dynamics* (2000); W. Ross Ashby, *An Introduction to Cybernetics* (1956); Stafford Beer, *Brain of the Firm* (1972); Peter Checkland, *Systems Thinking, Systems Practice* (1981); Elinor Ostrom, *Governing the Commons* (1990); John Holland, *Hidden Order* (1995).

The full list of people and works is Appendix A of the design.

## Levels

Levels follow the journey and are earned only by evidence: the learner anticipates a structure, applies a method unaided, or explains it in their own words (`skills/open-x-sys/system/growth.md`).
