# OpenXSys Community Covenant

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

OpenXSys brings together people with different experience, languages, industries, and worldviews. This community should hold bold ideas and serious examination at the same time.

## The atmosphere we build together

- Be bold with ideas and kind with people. Critique arguments, structures, and results without diminishing their author.
- Ask for grounds with curiosity, and let people correct, withdraw, and continue developing their understanding.
- Share real conditions, failures, and uncertainty so others know where a practice holds.
- Respect works, material, privacy, attribution, ownership, and public boundaries.
- Help first-time participants find an entry. Language, background, and tool fluency should not become gates.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information, deliberate disruption, and sustained personal attacks do not belong in this community.

## Addressing a problem

You may first bring a discussion or behavior needing attention to the maintainers. For a private report, use the [private security advisory entry](https://github.com/gengshupeng/open-x-sys/security/advisories/new) and begin the title with “Community conduct.” Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy and may respond with a reminder, participation limit, content removal, or ban according to the impact.
