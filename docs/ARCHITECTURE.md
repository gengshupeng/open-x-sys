# The Essence of OpenXSys and Its Natural-Language System Architecture

[简体中文](ARCHITECTURE.zh-CN.md)

**Every protagonist has a system. Now you do too.**

OpenXSys is a **recursive natural-language open-system runtime** inside AI tools and computer environments. A person says the part they have already reached. Behind the scenes, AI understands the system, organizes or creates capability, acts on reality, and absorbs feedback. The user receives a discovery that changes understanding, a genuinely finished work, and capability that remains useful later.

The runtime carries modeling, state, and composition internally. The foreground stays simple, free, and alive.

[Complete Skill](../skills/open-x-sys/SKILL.md) · [Open-X-Sys design draft](OPEN-X-SYS-DRAFT.md) · [Installation](INSTALL.md)

## Begin with one ordinary sentence

A user may say only:

> I want to study the entire artificial-intelligence industry.

They can begin before knowing how to divide the industry, which method to select, or what format the result should take. OpenXSys receives the sentence, recovers existing systems and material, actively finds the structure worth pursuing, and makes the understanding into a work native to the subject.

A complete experience makes four things real:

- **Surprise**: a previously hidden relation or unexpected finished result appears.
- **Freedom**: the person can question, correct, redirect, participate, and take over.
- **Gain**: they leave with something they can understand, use, and continue creating from.
- **Growth**: this gain changes the next run.

System metamodels, relation semantics, runtime loops, and dependency propagation live backstage. When someone opens the panorama, the grounds, or the design, those structures appear in human-readable form.

## Two structures that are true at once

### Physical containment

```text
Computer system
├── hardware, operating system, files, processes, network
├── AI host
│   ├── conversation, context, events
│   ├── LLM
│   ├── tools, permissions, execution
│   └── returned state
├── OpenXSys natural-language runtime
└── user-owned systems, works, data, and tools
```

People enter through devices and AI tools. Remote models, software, organizations, and devices connect through files, networks, interfaces, and human action.

### Actual relations of effect

```text
People, AI, computers, real subjects, project systems, independent systems
          ↕ observation, meaning, action, authority, results, feedback
                       OpenXSys active system
```

Physical reality nests; effects form a network. A model can run on another computer. A person can be the subject under study. A real system continues to operate by its own mechanisms. OpenXSys organizes the relations through which these systems produce actual effects on one another.

## The true center: a runnable system instance

Every run centers on the currently active system rather than on a prompt, method, or conversation turn.

It may be a company, industry, city, project, codebase, or a person's work. It may also be a capability, a relation, or a higher-level system temporarily formed by independent systems.

A system carries at least the semantics below. They are questions the AI can understand, while the user continues in ordinary language.

| Aspect | What must become clear |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity and purpose** | What it is, whom it serves, and which present result has value |
| **Boundary and scale** | Where this run looks, how many levels it opens, and across what span of time |
| **Reality, observation, model** | What the subject is doing, what was seen, what is inferred, and what remains unknown |
| **Components and relations** | Which parts participate and which relations truly transmit effects |
| **Constraints and resources** | What limits the whole, which conditions can change, and where the constraint may move |
| **Capability** | What it can do, what is validated, and what the purpose still requires |
| **Ownership and authority** | Who owns state, material, works, decisions, and execution |
| **Works and action** | How understanding reaches a person and intention enters reality |
| **Feedback and evolution** | What can change model, capability, relation, boundary, or goal |

The present run forms an active picture. Long-lived value is carried by `SYSTEM.md`, works, code, data, and connections. Persistence follows future usefulness and real evidence.

## Systems are recursive

In OpenXSys, anything that determines results can open at the scale where it becomes a system.

- A component opens into a system when its internal operation determines the outcome.
- A relation becomes a relation system when it includes observation, translation, authority, and feedback.
- A capability becomes an independent system when it gains state, boundary, and evolution.
- A work repeatedly used with outcome feedback grows into capability.
- Systems producing real effects around a shared purpose form a higher-level system.

After understanding a part, return to the whole and see how it changes the purpose above. A change of scale exists to locate the structure that keeps producing the visible event.

> **A structure at a higher level appears below as a problem that keeps coming back.**

## Relation: the smallest living structure

Systems are alive through relations that can produce effects. An important relation can answer:

```text
who acts on whom
what passes between them
how the receiver interprets or transforms it
under which conditions it works
who owns state and decision rights
how long the effect takes
what should change
how acceptance, execution, state, and effect are confirmed
where failure returns
how to pause, replace, withdraw, or leave
```

A **gulf** exists when a distinction, understanding, or capability held by one system cannot create a usable effect in another.

To bridge it, OpenXSys identifies whether observation, translation, action, coordination, or feedback is missing and organizes a bridge system. That system can combine people, AI, natural-language agreements, existing tools, generated code, shared data, and a new division of work. The bridge itself can be tested and evolved.

## Capability: a relational structure validated by reality

Models, data, and tools become capability when their relations can repeatedly transform an input or state into a valuable result under stated conditions, observe the effect, detect failure, and reorganize.

```text
purpose requires an outcome
        ↓
find the capability gulf the current system cannot cross
        ↓
reuse, adapt, compose, or create an implementation
        ↓
test with samples, counterexamples, authority, and real work
        ↓
connect it to the system with its operating conditions
        ↓
reuse, retest, replace, or keep evolving it
```

Generated code is a candidate implementation. Passing an isolated test makes it tested. Completing the user's real work with corresponding confirmation makes it a validated capability.

## Runtime: three loops composed freely

OpenXSys maintains three interlocking loops that can enter anywhere, run in parallel, jump, and return.

```text
Understanding loop: reality → observation → model → prediction or counterexample → revision

Action loop: purpose → organize capability → actual authority → execution → state and effect

Building loop: capability gulf → adapt, compose, or create → real validation → connection and reuse
```

The runtime loop changes this result. The building loop changes what the system will be able to do.

An action also carries five distinguishable states: proposed by the model, accepted by the host or target, actually executed, target state changed, intended effect observed. Different systems may confirm them at different times. A command's completion and a real-world effect are separate evidence.

A run can enter through human expression, file change, tool result, due signal, or another system's request. A returning host checks changes when the user comes back; an event-capable host can deliver them as they occur.

## Evolution: future behavior is genuinely different

Evolution is visible in what the next run sees and does.

When a lower-level failure repeats, diagnosis rises:

```text
state or chance event
→ current way of acting
→ missing or stale capability
→ relation, feedback, or authority design
→ higher-level resource structure, boundary, or goal
```

Higher-level change then moves downward:

```text
new purpose, model, or structure
→ changed capability requirements
→ changed components, relations, and authority
→ changed action
→ newly observed state and effect
```

New evidence updates the understanding, works, capabilities, and relations it actually touches. User edits and unaffected work remain in force. When a dependency fails, the system can locate downstream parts due for review, those still usable, and those currently unknown.

## Natural language, LLMs, computers, and reality

| Part | Role in the system |
| --- | --- |
| **Natural language** | Carries purpose, meaning, models, conditions, exceptions, rights, and quality in a form people and AI can jointly read and change |
| **LLM** | Interprets the present situation, constructs the active system picture, researches, projects, organizes, and creates |
| **Code and tools** | Carry precise, repeatable, testable observation, transformation, and execution |
| **AI host** | Supplies context, events, authority, tool calls, and returned results |
| **Domain system** | Retains its authoritative state, execution rules, and internal freedom |
| **Real outcomes** | Decide whether an explanation holds, an action worked, and a capability exists |

Natural language is the system's portable semantic source. Machine indexes can be generated from it while the user continues to work through meaning.

## How the components divide the work

| Component | Core responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Shared core** | Identity, initiative, experience promise, system definition, truth, ownership, and quality boundaries |
| **Active-system runner** | Recover the current system, construct the active picture, select scale, organize work, and process change |
| **Understanding system** | Observe, model, test, and maintain the two shores of understanding |
| **Capability organizer** | Decide among direct completion, reuse, composition, creation, and human responsibility |
| **Relation system** | Let distinctions and capabilities create real effects across boundaries |
| **Execution and effect layer** | Host authority, computer tools, external action, state confirmation, effect confirmation |
| **Works and experience** | Turn deep understanding into results people can see, use, and take over |
| **Evolution layer** | Raise repeated problems to structure, propagate dependency change, establish capability, differentiate and compose systems |

The first run activates what it needs. Components become persistent parts of the user's system when they create lasting value.

## Works: where deep models reach people

A work is the bridge between AI's understanding of a subject and a person's genuine understanding.

A complete work has a subject-native center strong enough to change understanding or action. An industry may become a world one can enter. A company may become a control structure. A codebase may become an execution anatomy. Repeated work may directly become a tool. Form serves discovery and use.

Interaction appears where it lets someone compare, test, or see dynamics. A complex work provides a panorama, present location, and a route back to the whole. When controls and decoration are removed, the central discovery still carries force. The first screen brings the real discovery before project explanation.

System events stay brief and name real transitions: discovery, connection, completed work, validated capability, system update, return. Ordinary search, file reading, and tool calls remain backstage.

## User-owned systems

Every long-lived system has a natural-language entry, `SYSTEM.md`. Its shape follows the subject. A new person or AI reading it can discover what the system is, whom and what it serves, how it is understood, its grounds and unknowns, what it can do, where important works and capabilities live, what it connects to, what matters now, and how to continue.

It preserves system state that changes future work: object understanding, the person's actual understanding, user corrections, works, validated capabilities, important relations, operating conditions, and the next starting point.

The user can read, change, carry, share, and end the system. Personal, project, domain, and external systems each retain their own state and boundary.

## An open world of systems

A higher-level system forms when independent systems create new whole capability around a shared purpose.

Each subsystem retains:

- ownership and authoritative state;
- internal freedom;
- authority boundaries;
- correction and exit rights.

The shared layer carries the relations, semantics, provenance, validation, and feedback needed for real collaboration. A work, understanding, capability, or subsystem from one party can be adopted, adapted, and validated by another, and actual feedback returns to whoever can improve it.

## Implementation locations

[`SKILL.md`](../skills/open-x-sys/SKILL.md) is the complete natural-language runtime entry. It preserves identity, experience, system view, initiative, work standard, real authority, and enough runtime semantics to begin on its own. Complex situations load the seven layers as needed:

| Layer | Location | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared core | [`core/`](../skills/open-x-sys/core/) | Constitution and invariants for system, relation, capability, and evolution; epistemology and systems science as deep resources |
| System runtime | [`runtime/`](../skills/open-x-sys/runtime/) | Activate, recover, compose, observe, act, verify, update; these responsibilities jump, recurse, and run in parallel |
| System instance | [`system/`](../skills/open-x-sys/system/) | Instance, state, ownership, composition, continuity, and detailed forms of the natural-language entry |
| Capability engines | [`engines/`](../skills/open-x-sys/engines/) | Understand, explore, express, build, evolve |
| Capability resources | [`capabilities/`](../skills/open-x-sys/capabilities/) | Methods, patterns, and practitioner routes that strengthen judgment |
| Environment adapters | [`adapters/`](../skills/open-x-sys/adapters/) | Connect the real boundaries of hosts, files, tools, Web, people, and external systems |
| Human experience | [`experience/`](../skills/open-x-sys/experience/) | Voice, works, events, panorama, return, display resources, and the Playground |

`agents/` and `assets/` supply host-discovery metadata and brand resources. The seven layers act together around one active system instance.

## Final test

When OpenXSys runs well, someone unfamiliar with systems thinking says one ordinary sentence and receives a discovery that changes understanding, a genuinely finished work, or capability they did not have before. They feel in command of direction while the system carries the internal complexity.

When a class of problem repeats, the system changes the higher-level structure producing it. When capability is missing, the system organizes or creates it and validates it in reality. A new conversation, model, or system can continue. Systems can compose greater capability and retain their freedom to leave.

> **A system runs inside. The user lives their own story.**
