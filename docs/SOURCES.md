# Sources and interpretation

[Chinese](SOURCES.zh-CN.md) · [Project introduction](../README.md)

OpenXSys draws on systems science and practitioners' ways of thinking. Each [method page](../skills/open-x-sys/methods) and [master route](../skills/open-x-sys/routes/masters) names its sources and explains the scope of its interpretation.

These include systems work by Wiener, Ashby, Forrester, Simon, von Bertalanffy, Beer, Senge and Meadows; and approaches associated with Ohno, Deming, Goldratt, Munger, Boyd, Feynman and others.

## Read the source in context

A route may be OpenXSys's arrangement of ideas. It is identified as such where applicable. Examples and uncertain attributions keep their specific source notes on the relevant page.

For research outputs, distinguish source statements, estimates, interpretations and scenarios. Keep dates, units, source links and the reasoning behind important conclusions.

## The featured industry report

The [AI-industry case](../gallery/ai-industry/README.md) records an actual output supplied by the author. Its English page translates the original report; its figures, judgments and scenarios belong to that dated artifact. The run notes identify its tool, model and prompt.

## Contributing a correction

Open an issue or pull request with the page, the statement, a primary source and the proposed correction. Update the complete Chinese edition along with the English edition.
