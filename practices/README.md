# Practices

[Chinese](README.zh-CN.md)

Practices that people proved in their own work and chose to share, each with its conditions and evidence.

**There are no shared practices yet.** A practice is listed only when someone has actually used it and recorded what happened.

## What a shared practice contains

A shared practice is a practice record (see `skills/open-x-sys/engines/practice.md`) with everything personal removed. It lets a reader judge whether it fits them:

- what the practice is: its parts, connections, order and triggers;
- the goal it serves;
- **the conditions it was used under**: time available, budget, skills, tools, scale;
- the evidence, and its kind: measured, reported, or argued;
- the cost;
- results, with dates;
- switching conditions: when to keep, adjust or replace it;
- lineage, if it was adapted from another shared practice.

## Rules

- A practice without its conditions is generic advice and is not listed.
- Results are reported as they were, including what did not work.
- A practice is not a script. It stops at principles and the few decisions that determine success.
- Nothing personal: no names, employers, private paths or connection details.
