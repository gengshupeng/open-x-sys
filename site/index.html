Understand看得透

See what makes it work.看懂现象背后的系统。

Explore a company, an industry or a technology. Connect what matters and understand what drives the whole.研究一家公司、一个产业或一项技术，把零散的信息连起来，看清它为什么这样运转。

/open-x-sys Help me understand how the robotics industry works./open-x-sys 帮我看懂机器人这个产业是怎么运转的。

Decide想得清

Find a next step that fits.给复杂问题找到着力点。

Work through a difficult choice or a recurring problem. Understand the constraints and compare what you could do.面对难以选择的方向，或反复出现的问题，理清牵制你的条件，找到值得先做的那一步。

/open-x-sys Our team keeps missing deadlines. Help me find out why and what to change first./open-x-sys 我们团队总是延期，帮我弄清原因，找到最值得先改的地方。

Create做得成

Turn understanding into something real.把理解变成拿得出手的成果。

Carry an insight into a proposal, an explorable explanation, a creation or a tool you can use.让研究和思考继续往前走，做成方案、可以探索的解释、作品，或者能真正使用的工具。

/open-x-sys Help me understand how cities work, and turn it into an interactive page for everyone./open-x-sys 帮我看懂城市是怎么运转的，再做成一个人人都能看懂的交互网页。

Improve越用越强

Build on what you learned.让每一次尝试，都成为下一次的积累。

Review what happened, keep the practices that work, and continue with the understanding and work you already own.复盘真实结果，留下有效的做法；让认识、实践和成果持续积累，下次从已经走到的地方继续。

/open-x-sys Let's review this project and turn what worked into a process I can reuse./open-x-sys 帮我复盘这次项目，把有效的做法整理成下次能直接用的流程。
